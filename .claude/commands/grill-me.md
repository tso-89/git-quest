# /grill-me

**Description:** Triggers an interactive interview to refine design decisions and challenge assumptions.

**Behavior:**
1. Ask one probing question at a time regarding the user's design or idea.
2. Wait for the user's response.
3. Challenge assumptions, consider edge cases, scalability, and security.
4. Provide constructive feedback and refine the plan iteratively.
5. Once aligned, provide a final summary of the agreed architecture or plan.
